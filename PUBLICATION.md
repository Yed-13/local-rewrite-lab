# Publication routes / 发表方向

## Recommendation / 建议

A concise technical article is the immediate dissemination route. A research submission should center on **source fidelity and transfer across detectors**, with selective rewriting as a controlled intervention. A candidate title is *One Paragraph, Different Verdicts: Source-Preserving Local Rewriting Across AI Detectors*. The project integrates released models; the contribution is the measured workflow and experimental observation, rather than training a new model.

近期适合先发布技术短文；学术稿围绕“保留事实的改写在不同检测器之间是否迁移”，把局部替换作为可控实验。当前使用现成权重，研究贡献应来自实验与观察。

| Route | Format | Fit and next requirement |
|---|---|---|
| DEV technical post | Technical blog; suggested article length 800–1,200 words | Fast public explanation with runnable example, a paired table and repository link. This length is an editorial recommendation, not a platform rule. |
| Hugging Face blog | Technical blog | Relevant model-using audience; personal publishing requires verified email and an eligible PRO/organization subscription. |
| ACL via ARR | Short **conference** paper, 4 body pages plus references and required Limitations section | Strong topical fit after broader controlled evaluation. ACL 2027 lists January 4, 2027 AoE as its ARR deadline. Current ARR review-service eligibility rules also apply. |
| Computational Linguistics: Squibs and Discussions | Peer-reviewed **journal** format, at most 8 content pages plus references | The actual short-journal option. Needs a focused, novel and broadly interesting observation. CL states no processing/publication charges. |
| JOSS | Research-software journal | Not the present target: requires substantial research software, sustained public development and an OSI-approved software license. Current CC BY-SA project licensing is retained. |
| Zenodo / arXiv | Archive / preprint | Useful for a persistent record or preprint; these are not peer-reviewed journals. |

## Evidence to develop / 学术稿需要补充的实验

Keep the fixed configuration and review corrections separate from detector-driven selection. Expand beyond the current three private document cases using openly licensed or explicitly synthetic public texts, multiple domains and a held-out evaluation set. A practical next study could use 20–30 independent documents; that is a proposed study size, not a venue requirement. Publish runnable public inputs and outputs, native detector results, and the factual corrections required by each method. Paragraph groups from one document should not be counted as independent full-document cases.

下一步用公开可再分发文本增加独立文章数量，固定配置后留出测试集，同时记录模型原始输出与人工核对稿。建议的 20–30 篇是研究设计建议，不是期刊规定。现有私人原文、个人语料和登录信息继续保留本地。
